About Reverie

Long description

Reverie is an award-winning psychedelic psychological horror action-adventure set in a world where reality is breaking apart.

Explore a strange, hazardous, procedurally generated open world filled with infected creatures, crumbling structures, hidden abstract worlds, and fragments of a larger plot that threatens the entire world. Fight your way through infested dungeons, discover new abilities, collect weapons and resources, craft alien drugs, and meet strangers who you grow too invested in.

You are not merely trying to survive. You are trying to understand what brought you to this place.

Death eludes you as you fight against the strings of fate. Each failure pushes you deeper into Reverie's systems, your past, and the strings that guide you. Experiment with and replace your weapons to claw towards the truth of what's going on around you.

Gameplay & themes

Core pillars and feature copy from the game's store description.

Fight. Die. Return.

Combat will leave you on the edge of your seat as you perfect your spacing and learn the enemies' habits. Cleave through creatures with a variety of weapons. Enhance and modify equipment with elemental effects, combine gear to optimize your playstyle, and find abilities to travel the world with and dominate the battlefield. Evolving environments, creatures, and skills will push you to your limits.

Explore a cruel, forsaken world

Reverie's world focuses on natural exploration, discovery, and the slow realization that rock bottom can be smashed through. Collect materials, gear, monster remains, and other rare resources as you travel. Explore labyrinthine laboratories, uncover hidden areas, follow your gut, and find the threads that tie your fate to the heart of this world. Meet friends who help you along your journey, and discern those who can't be trusted. Some stones are best left untouched. Unfortunately, sometimes the only way to truly learn is through experience.

Mind over matter

What happens to you changes you. Reverie tracks your character's emotions as you play. Fear, anger, sadness, happiness, delusion, stoicism, and numbness affect how you approach combat and see the world.

Death is inevitable

Return from failure stronger, unlock new possibilities, and face the past you've been running from, as every mistake reminds you of what you are. The more you die, the tighter the strings of fate tie around your neck.

Play alone or with friends

Experience Reverie as a single-player narrative or get lost with friends in the mirage with online co-op. Fight, explore, and discover together a world that tries to break you down even if you're not alone.

Features

  • Psychedelic psychological horror set in a world warped by your own mind
  • Fast-paced hack-and-slash combat
  • Single-player and online co-op
  • Exploration of a vast procedurally generated open world
  • Weapon upgrades, crafting, and trading
  • Unlockable skills and acquirable abilities that expand combat and exploration
  • Replayable systems and procedural elements
  • A unique emotion system that affects mood, discovery, and combat
  • Death that feeds into the narrative and progression
  • A story that questions who is in control, the reality it's set in, and the ability of the human spirit to overcome oneself
